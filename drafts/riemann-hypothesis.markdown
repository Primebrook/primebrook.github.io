## The Riemann Zeta Function

The Riemann zeta function is defined for $\text{Re}(s) > 1$ as:

$$
\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^s}
$$

## The Hypothesis

The Riemann Hypothesis states that all non-trivial zeros of $\zeta(s)$ have real part equal to $\frac{1}{2}$.
